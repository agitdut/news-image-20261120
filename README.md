Entry for make.fun's `news-image-20261120` bounty ($250, program/v1): one
image summing up the world's news of Friday 20 November 2026.

The image is a dense single front page (`draw_news.py`, Pillow), with a
masthead naming the day, the Shenzhen trilateral beside the Hague genocide
ruling, three features (Kyiv, Anthropic's debut, Washington), three columns
of short items, a markets strip and a sources footer — about 22 stories in
all, each reported on 20 November 2026:

1. **Trump, Putin and Xi issue a 'peace declaration'** — first-ever
   trilateral talks at APEC in Shenzhen; sources call it vague, with no
   significant agreements on Ukraine or nuclear arms.
2. **World court: Myanmar committed genocide** — the ICJ finds Myanmar
   guilty of genocide against the Rohingya, 9–6, the first such ruling in
   the court's 80-year history.
3. **Russian missiles hit Kyiv bridges** — 3 killed, 9 injured; several
   bridges damaged, power cut; the EU summons Russia's envoy over embassy
   damage.
4. **Anthropic soars on Nasdaq debut** — the first purely AI company to
   list; shares jump, valuing it near $2 trillion.
5. **Senate rejects Iran war-powers bid, 51–48** — the Pentagon puts the
   Iran war's cost at $48.3 billion; Trump pardons Pete Rose.
6. Short items: UK pledges 1,000+ drones for Ukraine (£260m); Poland
   probes unknown objects; Genoa airport halts flights over drones;
   Houthis claim four Saudi targets; tanker on fire after a mine strike
   east of Hormuz; quake 5.6 near New Caledonia; gas blast kills six in
   Georgia; fire in a French tower block; Israeli strike in Lebanon;
   UK–EU youth mobility; Harris meets Macron; Las Vegas GP practice
   washed out; Fiona the hippo dies; US sanctions Comancheros and the
   'Ndrangheta; Argentina backs a disability law — plus Thursday's market
   closes (S&P 500 −2.02%, Dow −2.19%, Nasdaq −2.25%, Brent $91.98,
   Bitcoin ~$62,150).

Sources: AP, Reuters, BBC, Le Monde, Investopedia.

The program (`main.ts`) copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`.
The file is a PNG under 1 MiB, 1024 by 1024, within the gate's limits.
